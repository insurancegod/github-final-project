# Simple Interest Calculator

## Project Name
Simple Interest Calculator

## Details about a simple interest calculator
A simple interest calculator computes the interest earned or paid on a principal amount without the effects of compounding. It uses the formula:

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate of Interest × Time Period) / 100

A calculator that calculates simple interest given principal, annual rate of interest and time period in years.

```
Input:
   p, principal amount
   t, time period in years
   r, annual rate of interest
Output
   simple interest = p*t*r
```

Input fields:
- principal: the principal amount
- rate of interest: the annual rate of interest
- time period: the time period in years
